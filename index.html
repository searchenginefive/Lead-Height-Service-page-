Frequently Asked Questions

Straight answers to what businesses ask us most about digital marketing services.

What are digital marketing services?

Digital marketing services are the activities a business uses to attract and convert customers online. They include search engine optimisation (SEO), Google Ads and other pay-per-click advertising, social media marketing, content marketing, email and WhatsApp marketing, website development and online reputation management. An agency plans these channels, runs them and measures the results against your business goals.

How can digital marketing help my business grow?

It puts your business in front of people who are already looking for what you sell, at the moment they’re ready to act. Search and ads bring in new enquiries, social media and content build familiarity and trust, and analytics show which activity produced each lead or sale, so you can invest more in what works and cut what doesn’t.

How much do digital marketing services cost in India?

Costs depend on the channels you need, how competitive your industry is, the cities you target and how fast you want to grow. A local business running local SEO and a small ad campaign needs a very different budget from a national brand running SEO, paid media and content together.

For paid campaigns, keep in mind that the agency’s management fee is separate from the ad spend you pay to Google or Meta. We recommend a budget after understanding your goals, and you can request a free proposal with a clear cost breakdown.

Which digital marketing services are best for small businesses?

Most small businesses get the quickest return from local SEO, a well-managed Google Business Profile, a fast mobile-friendly website and tightly targeted Google or Meta ads. Once these are producing steady enquiries, content marketing and broader SEO help lower your cost per customer over time.

How long does it take to see results from digital marketing?

Paid advertising such as Google Ads and Meta Ads can start bringing enquiries within days of launch, then improves over the first few weeks as campaigns are optimised. SEO usually takes three to six months to show meaningful ranking and traffic gains, depending on your website’s current state and competition. Content and reputation work keep compounding well beyond that.

What is the difference between SEO and PPC advertising?

SEO improves your website so it ranks in Google’s free, organic results. It takes longer to build but keeps delivering traffic without paying for each click. PPC advertising, such as Google Ads, places paid listings at the top of results right away, and you pay each time someone clicks. Many businesses use PPC for immediate leads while SEO builds long-term visibility.

Why should I hire a digital marketing agency in India?

An agency gives you a team of specialists across SEO, ads, design, content and analytics for less than the cost of hiring each role in-house. An India-based agency also understands local search behaviour, regional languages and festivals, platform costs and how Indian customers compare and buy.

Does Lead Height provide digital marketing services across India?

Yes. Lead Height is based in Kolkata and works with businesses in Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Bengaluru, Hyderabad, Ahmedabad, Pune and other cities across India. Strategy calls, reporting and campaign management are all handled online, so your location doesn’t limit what we can do.

Can digital marketing agencies help generate quality leads?

Yes, when campaigns are built for quality rather than volume. That means targeting high-intent keywords and audiences, using landing pages and forms that qualify enquiries, tracking which leads become customers, and feeding that data back into the campaigns. We measure success by qualified leads and sales, not just form fills.

How do I choose the right digital marketing company for my business?

Look for experience in your industry, a clear strategy rather than a generic package, transparent reporting, and ownership of your ad accounts and data staying with you. Ask how they measure success, who will manage your account day to day, and what results they’ve achieved for similar businesses.
